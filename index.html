Scoring Tips & Instructions

  • Don't overthink it. If you're torn between a 3 and a 4, pick one and move on—the goal is to compare palettes, not perfect precision.
  • Score one question across all 9 palettes first (not one palette across all questions)—it helps your brain calibrate what a "3" vs "5" actually means.
  • Flexibility testing: For each palette, open Canva and click "shuffle" a few times on the brand kit to see how the colors work in different compositions. We're testing flexibility too—does the palette maintain its integrity when colors are arranged differently?
  • Saving: Click "Save Scores" after you're done scoring to save your data locally. Click "Export CSV" to download all scores for analysis.
